# UI audit map, 2026-11-09

Scope: the Android app at v0.1.340 (`android/app/src/main/kotlin/nl/baskt/ui`), every screen, sheet, dialog and widget.
Method: all UI code was read, and the release build was walked through on a phone in dark theme (basket, overflow, item detail, plan, orders, recipes, deals, tidy, search, stock, receipts, shop mode, assistant, settings, product sheet).
Items marked **(seen)** were confirmed on the device. Items marked **(check)** are layout predictions from dp values. Everything else was read in the code.
Paths are relative to `ui/` unless they start with another folder. Severity: **H** breaks a task or misleads, **M** friction or visible inconsistency, **L** polish.

Deliberate decisions were not flagged: Assistant FAB bottom-left plus Scan FAB, merged Plan page, TripStrip, small top bar on the three pager pages, white till screens, Settings grouping, single "Scan receipt" FAB.

### 2.2 Same component built several ways

- **Steppers**: three hand-rolled versions with different layouts and labels ("One more"/"One less", "Less"/"More", "Fewer"/"More"), plus a fourth in self-scan and a fifth in Settings. `AddIdeaBar.kt:176-180`, `ItemDetailScreen.kt:214-219`, `GroupScreen.kt:131-136`, `SelfScan.kt:340-351`, `SettingsScreen.kt:159-161`.
- **Search fields**: Deals (trailing button), Recipes (leading icon), Stock (pill, hidden behind a toggle), Search (field plus 40dp button), Item detail. None is an M3 `SearchBar`, none has a clear button, most leave the keyboard up after submit. `DealsScreen.kt:225-234`, `RecipesScreen.kt:150-159`, `StockScreen.kt:171-179`, `SearchScreen.kt:88-97`.
- **Text field shape**: pill in Tidy and Stock (seen), default everywhere else. `CommentBar.kt:61`, `StockScreen.kt:171`.
- **List rows**: `Card` rows (basket), flat rows with dividers (shop mode, seen), `ListItem` (tidy), custom `ProductRow`, hand-rolled settings rows. Shop mode alone has three row paddings (`ShopModeScreen.kt:266`, `:298`, `:367`).
- **Read-only tags**: four hand-rolled versions in shop mode and self-scan instead of `Tag`. `ShopModeScreen.kt:330-337`, `:452-461`, `SelfScan.kt:271-273`, `:357-361`. `AssistChip` used as a button at `BasketScreen.kt:430`, `FilterChip` as a sheet opener at `DealsScreen.kt:242`, hand-rolled `SelectableChip` at `AddIdeaBar.kt:194-209`.
- **Pickers**: Settings pickers are `OutlinedButton` plus `DropdownMenu` with no arrow, label or selected mark, so "Español", "MiniMax Turbo" and "Powerful Soldier" look like actions (seen). `SettingsScreen.kt:172-182`, `:233-237`, `SpeechSettings.kt:49-70`.
- **Single choice**: segmented buttons (Settings, Deals, Recipes, Tidy), toggle buttons (Plan), filter chips (till interval, receipt store). M3 Expressive uses a connected `ButtonGroup`.
- **Product-row Add**: `FilledTonalButton` "Add" (convention), icon-only `AddShoppingCart` (`OrdersScreen.kt:302`, `SimilarSheet.kt:179`), filled `Button` "Add to list" (`ScannerScreen.kt:204`), filled "Use this deal" on every deal card (`DealsScreen.kt:173`).
- **Empty-state action**: `FilledTonalButton`, `Button` and `TextButton` in different screens. `DealsScreen.kt:148`, `MyBonusTab.kt:54`, `RecipesScreen.kt:401`, `StockScreen.kt:163`, `BasketScreen.kt:331`. Several empty states are plain text instead of `EmptyState` (`SelfScan.kt:252`, `ScannerScreen.kt:137`, `ChatScreen.kt:160`, `AhLoginScreen.kt:77`).
- **Loading**: `LoadingIndicator` plus text, `LinearWavyProgressIndicator` alone, plain text, or nothing, sometimes within one screen (`DealsScreen.kt:137-140`, `:269`, `MyBonusTab.kt:58`). `LoadingIndicator` is squeezed to 16 to 22dp in many rows.
- **Errors**: global snackbar, per-screen snackbar (Basket, Deals, Shop, Search have their own host, so two can show at different heights), inline grey text, inline red text, `Toast` (`BarcodeScan.kt:40`, `PurchasesScreen.kt:103`), raw exception or server strings.
- **Destructive actions**: confirm dialog (item, folder, basket, recipe, receipt), undo snackbar (shop mode), nothing (learned preference, self-scan line, loyalty card, "Paid · clear codes", offline change discard, thumbs-down, remove step, unfavourite, "Remove from stock" in the scanner). Five delete confirmations use five phrasings (`BasketScreen.kt:160`, `:168`, `GroupScreen.kt:65`, `ItemDetailScreen.kt:109`, `Baskets.kt:90`).
- **Sheet layout**: the 20dp sides and 32dp bottom rule is broken in `ProductSheet.kt:125`, `SimilarSheet.kt:100` (20dp all round, forced 92% height, left-aligned "Close" button), `DealsScreen.kt:251` (24dp), `SelfScan.kt:215-320` (16 and 20 mixed, 24 bottom). Action rows are left-aligned with primary first in `DealsScreen.kt:171-178`, `:377-380`, `RecipesScreen.kt:203-210`, `RecipeEditorScreen.kt:107-110`, `ChatScreen.kt:290-293`, `AddIdeaBar.kt:143-146`, centred in `DictationSheet.kt:114`.
- **Sheet title**: generic label above the real title in `ProductSheet.kt:127` ("Product details", seen) and `RecipeSheet.kt:87` ("Recipe"). No title at all in the scan result sheet, receipt detail sheet and self-scan basket sheet.
- **Stacked modals**: product sheet over scan sheet over similar sheet over product sheet (`Components.kt:102`, `ProductSheet.kt:149`), image dialog over sheet, stop dialog over self-scan sheet, receipt editor over detail sheet, "Transcribing…" dialog after the dictation sheet (`VoiceInput.kt:76`).
- **Top bars**: small bar on stack list screens that should use the medium flexible one (Folder, Search, Shop mode, Assistant). Tidy closes with X while every other pushed screen uses a back arrow (seen). Orders and Plan show a back arrow although they are pager pages (seen), with "Back to the list" vs "Back" elsewhere.
